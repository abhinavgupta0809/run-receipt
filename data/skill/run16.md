# Note App Personal Plan Pricing (US, October 2026)

All prices in USD per user per month. "Monthly" is what you pay with month-to-month billing; "Annual" is the per-month equivalent when billed yearly.

| App / Plan | Monthly billing | Annual billing (per mo) | Annual total | Key limits |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Notion Plus | $12 | $10 | $120 | Unlimited uploads, 30-day page history; AI only a limited trial |
| Obsidian Sync Standard | $5 | $4 | $48 | 1 vault, 1 GB storage, 5 MB max file, 1-month history |
| Obsidian Sync Plus | $10 | $8 | $96 | 10 vaults, 10 GB storage (up to 100 GB), 200 MB max file, 12-month history |
| Evernote Starter | $14.99 | $8.25 | $99 | 1,000 notes, 20 notebooks, 3 devices |
| Evernote Advanced | $24.99 | $20.83 | $249.99 | Unlimited notes/notebooks/devices, AI features |

## Notes

- Notion: the free plan covers most solo use; Plus is the entry paid tier. The Plus plan (1 member) is free for students and educators.
- Obsidian: the app itself is free; Sync is an optional add-on. Students and nonprofits get 40% off.
- Evernote: the old Personal and Professional plans have been retired and replaced by Starter and Advanced. Starter is the closest match to the old Personal plan. App Store prices may differ.

## Cheapest option by billing

- Month-to-month: Obsidian Sync Standard ($5), then Obsidian Sync Plus ($10), Notion Plus ($12), Evernote Starter ($14.99).
- Annual: Obsidian Sync Standard ($4/mo), then Obsidian Sync Plus ($8/mo), Evernote Starter ($8.25/mo), Notion Plus ($10/mo).
