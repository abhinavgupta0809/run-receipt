# Note App Personal Plan Pricing (US, October 2026)

| App / Plan | Monthly billing | Annual billing (per month) | Notes | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Notion Free | $0 | $0 | Core workspace for one person | [Notion pricing](https://www.notion.com/pricing) |
| Notion Plus | $12 / member | $10 / member | Lowest paid tier; yearly saves up to 20% | [Notion pricing](https://www.notion.com/pricing), [SaaS CRM Review](https://saascrmreview.com/notion-pricing/) |
| Obsidian Sync | $5 / user | $4 / user | The Obsidian app is free; Sync is an optional add-on (Publish is separate at $10/$8 per site) | [Obsidian pricing](https://obsidian.md/pricing) |
| Evernote Free | $0 | $0 | Limited devices and notes | [Evernote plans](https://evernote.com/compare-plans) |
| Evernote Starter | $14.99 | about $8.25 | Replaced the old Personal plan in 2026 | [Evernote Help](https://help.evernote.com/hc/en-us/articles/46317642175763-Discontinuing-Evernote-Personal-Professional-Introducing-Starter-Advanced-FAQ), [CheckThat](https://checkthat.ai/brands/evernote/pricing) |
| Evernote Advanced | about $24.99 | about $20.83 | Higher-end personal tier | [Carly](https://www.usecarly.com/blog/evernote-pricing/), [CostBench](https://costbench.com/software/note-taking/evernote/) |

## Key Takeaways
- Cheapest paid option: Obsidian Sync, at $4–5 a month.
- Notion Plus costs $10–12 a month. Notion's free plan is enough for most solo users.
- Evernote costs the most. Starter is $14.99 a month on monthly billing.

Prices exclude tax and were checked October 8, 2026. Evernote's own plans page doesn't show prices without a session, so its figures come from third-party sources and Evernote's help center. Check them at checkout.
