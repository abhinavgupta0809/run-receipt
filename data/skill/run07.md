# Notion vs Obsidian Sync vs Evernote: US Personal Plan Prices

Checked October 8, 2026. All prices in USD, before tax.

| App / plan | Billed monthly | Billed annually (per month) | What you get |
|---|---|---|---|
| Notion Free | $0 | $0 | Unlimited blocks for individuals, 5 MB uploads, 7-day page history |
| Notion Plus | $10 per member ([Notion](https://www.notion.com/pricing)) | Up to 20% less ([Notion](https://www.notion.com/pricing)) | Unlimited uploads, 30-day history, unlimited guests |
| Obsidian (app) | Free | Free | Full app, local files, no account needed |
| Obsidian Sync | $5 per user ([Obsidian](https://obsidian.md/pricing)) | $4 per user ([Obsidian](https://obsidian.md/pricing)) | End-to-end encrypted sync, version history, shared vaults |
| Evernote Free | $0 | $0 | 50 notes, 1 notebook, 1 device ([Evernote](https://evernote.com/compare-plans)) |
| Evernote Starter | ~$14.99 ([Carly](https://www.usecarly.com/blog/evernote-pricing/)) | ~$8.25 ([Costbench](https://costbench.com/software/note-taking/evernote/)) | 1,000 notes, 20 notebooks, 3 devices, 5 GB |
| Evernote Advanced | ~$24.99 ([Carly](https://www.usecarly.com/blog/evernote-pricing/)) | ~$20.83 ([Costbench](https://costbench.com/software/note-taking/evernote/)) | Unlimited notes, devices, and storage |

## Takeaways
- Cheapest paid option: Obsidian Sync at $5/month, or $4 if you pay yearly.
- Notion Plus costs about twice as much as Obsidian Sync, but it's a full workspace with collaboration built in.
- Evernote costs the most. In 2026 it replaced its Personal and Professional plans with Starter and Advanced ([Evernote Help](https://help.evernote.com/hc/en-us/articles/46317642175763-Discontinuing-Evernote-Personal-Professional-Introducing-Starter-Advanced-FAQ)).

## Caveats
- Notion's page shows $10 but doesn't say whether that's the monthly-billed or yearly rate. In the past, Notion listed the yearly rate by default and charged about $12 for month-to-month.
- Evernote's plan page doesn't show prices without a login or region check, so its numbers come from third-party trackers. Confirm at checkout.
